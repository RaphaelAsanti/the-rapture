The Two Raptures and the Community of Eternal Paradise

Shortly after Revelation was altered to include the term 'stadia' in the measurements of (and desecrating) God's Holy City , God Almighty took several actions in response. One of those actions involved the first of two raptures described in the Book of Revelation.

Of course, those who were responsible for altering the book of Revelation already lost any opportunity for God's Holy City and/or the 'tree of life described in that scroll' and are under additional curses. Their names were blotted out of the Book of Life on Rosh Hashanah and that was finalized on Yom Kippur.

For I testify unto every man that heareth the words of the prophecy of this book, If any man shall add unto these things, God shall add unto him the plagues that are written in this book: And if any man shall take away from the words of the book of this prophecy, God shall take away his part out of the book of life, and out of the holy city, and from the things which are written in this book.
Revelation 22:18-19

The First Rapture

The first rapture corresponds to the passages:

Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments, lest he walk naked, and they see his shame.
Revelation 16:15

This also corresponds to Christ's statement that if the owner of the house knew the time of the thief's arrival, he would have remained watchful.

But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what watch the thief would come, he would have watched and would not have suffered his house to be broken into.
Matthew 24:43

On July 22, 2025, God Almighty quietly gathered 7,000 souls who had never kissed Baal as He promised Elijah.

Yet I have left Me seven thousand in Israel, all the knees which have not bowed unto Baal, and every mouth which hath not kissed him.”
1 Kings 19:18

God also extended a special mercy to two additional souls and assigned them to a specific care giver who has never stepped foot inside this universe. That brought the total number taken in the first rapture to 7,002 souls.

These souls were removed by God's Own Hand, from back in time and granted lightbodies, homes, and citizenship within a new community established in the Eternal Paradise Universe. This gathering occurred quietly and without public recognition. God reached back through time and removed these souls from the earthly timeline and transported them to the eternal paradise universe where they will reside forever.

The Eternal Paradise Universe

The Eternal Paradise Universe is distinct from the present universe. The 7,002 souls taken in the first rapture currently reside there in their lightbodies . Because of the time differential (a day there is like 1,000 years here); they have only been there for not much than a few moments even though they were taken from here last year. God Almighty Himself has been watching over all of them while they sleep in their new homes there since He removed them from here.

Because the standard was lower than any other covenant, these souls still have much to learn about how to survive and thrive within the structure of the Eternal Paradise universe. God Almighty Himself serves as their only teacher until they reach spiritual adulthood. Although they still have much to learn, with God Almighty as their instructor they will not fail.

Some entered the community in adult form. Others entered as infants and children. All are spiritually young; those who entered as 'children' and even infants have the most to learn. Those who were given positions as adults serve as caregivers for the younger souls. Every soul within Eternal Paradise are equals and each is considered as one of God's children so the terms 'mother' and 'father' do not apply. They are caregivers, akin to nursemaids. They were chosen for these positions due to their own innate care-giving abilities - while they also learn the ways of the Eternal Paradise universe. The caregiver positions are positions of trust and respect.

The Second Rapture

A second rapture opportunity was later proclaimed through the call:

Then I heard another voice from heaven say, “Come out of her, my people, so that you do not participate in her sins, and so that you do not receive any of her plagues.
Revelation 18:4

Unlike the first rapture, this invitation was openly announced loudly at the very end instead of secretly and quietly like the first.

Additional positions had been made available for souls who might also serve as caregivers within the Eternal Paradise community. I have colloquially referred to these positions as "a seat at the table with the breast feeders".

The qualification for these positions was demonstrated ability to care for an orphan who is your equal under Go but cannot fend for himself. The test was practical rather than verbal. A person cannot claim the ability to care for another of God's Children while abandoning him to fend for himself, proclaiming yourself to be his 'mother' or 'father', claiming the right to discipline him and leaving him alone so you can go drinking and dancing with your friends while asserting your own superiority.

The candidates repeatedly demonstrated that they lacked the qualities required for caregiving another of God's Own Children as their equal in the Eternal Paradise universe. As a result, the available positions were closed and the opportunity ended.

As of the time of this writing, judgment is completed and the results are final. There will be no second rapture.
