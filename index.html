The Two Raptures and the Community of Eternal Paradise

 

Shortly after Revelation was altered to include the term 'stadia' in the measurements of God's Holy City, God Almighty took several actions in response. One of those actions involved the first of two raptures described in the Book of Revelation.

 

The First Rapture

 

The first rapture corresponds to the passages:

 
Behold, I come as a thief. Blessed is he that watcheth, and keepeth his garments, lest he walk naked, and they see his shame.
Revelation 16:15
 

This also corresponds to Christ's statement that if the owner of the house knew the time of the thief's arrival, he would have remained watchful.

 
But know this, that if the master of the house had known in what watch the thief would come, he would have watched and would not have suffered his house to be broken into.
Matthew 24:43
 

On July 22, 2025, God Almighty quietly gathered 7,000 souls who had never kissed Baal as He promised Elijah.

 
Yet I have left Me seven thousand in Israel, all the knees which have not bowed unto Baal, and every mouth which hath not kissed him.”
1 Kings 19:18

God also extended a special mercy to two additional souls.

 

The total number taken in this first rapture was therefore 7,002 souls.

 

These souls were granted lightbodies, homes, and citizenship within a new community established in the Eternal Paradise Universe.

 

This gathering occurred quietly and without public recognition. God reached back through time and removed these souls from the earthly timeline.

 

The Eternal Paradise Universe

 

The Eternal Paradise Universe is distinct from the present universe.

 

The 7,002 souls taken in the first rapture currently reside there.

 

God Himself serves as their teacher.

 

Some entered the community in adult form. Others entered as infants and children.

 

The adults serve as caregivers for the younger souls. Every soul within Eternal Paradise is considered one of God's children.

 

The Second Rapture

 

A second rapture opportunity was later announced through the call:

 
"Come out of her, my people, so that you do not participate in her sins."
 

Unlike the first rapture, this invitation was openly announced.

 

Additional places were available for souls who would serve as caregivers within the Eternal Paradise community.

 

The qualification for these positions was demonstrated ability to care for an orphan.

 

The test was practical rather than verbal. A person cannot claim to care for an orphan while abandoning him to fend for himself and leaving him alone while pursuing other interests.

 

The candidates repeatedly demonstrated that they lacked the qualities required for caregiving. As a result, the available positions were closed and the opportunity ended.

 

As of the time of this writing, judgment is completed and the results are final.
